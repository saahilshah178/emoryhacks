# Fall response

Practice guiding someone who is falling down to the floor, with a big pillow as the person. You never try to hold a falling person up: you slow them down.

## You need
- A big pillow, a couch cushion or a full backpack, as the person
- Clear floor about the size of a bed
- A TV remote on the floor nearby, as the phone you would call for help with

## Camera
Prop your phone about 8 feet away, to your side, so your whole body and the floor in front of you show.

## Safety
- Never practice this with a real person falling.
- Never try to hold a falling person up, or pull them back to standing.
- Bend your knees, not your back.
- Never lift a fallen person on your own. Call for help.

## Steps

### s1. Stand close behind
Hold the pillow upright in front of you, its back against your chest, with both arms around its middle.
Pass when: the stand-in is held upright against the caregiver's chest, with both arms around its middle.
Try again when: it is held out at arm's length, or by a corner.
Key step: yes

### s2. Feet wide, one leg forward
Step your feet wide apart and put one leg forward with the knee bent, so your thigh is under the pillow.
Pass when: the feet are wide apart with one leg forward and bent under the stand-in, and the back stays upright.
Try again when: the feet are together, or the caregiver bends at the waist with straight legs.
Key step: yes
After: s1

### s3. Slide them down your leg
Bend both knees and let the pillow slide slowly down your front leg to the floor. Do not drop it.
Pass when: the stand-in slides slowly down the front leg to the floor while the caregiver's knees bend.
Try again when: the stand-in is dropped or thrown down, or is pulled back up to standing.
Key step: yes
After: s2

### s4. Kneel beside them
Kneel next to the pillow and rest a hand on it. Do not lift it back up.
Pass when: the caregiver kneels or crouches beside the stand-in on the floor, with a hand on it.
Try again when: the caregiver walks away, or picks the stand-in back up.
After: s3

### s5. Call for help without leaving
Stay kneeling, pick up the remote and hold it to your ear to call for help.
Pass when: while still beside the stand-in, the caregiver picks something up and holds it to the ear as a phone.
Try again when: the caregiver leaves the stand-in to fetch help, or never calls.
After: s4
