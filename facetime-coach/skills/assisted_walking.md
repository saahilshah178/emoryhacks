# Assisted walking

Practice a safe short walk from one seat to another: clear the way, pause before the first step, and sit with control. You do the walk yourself. If someone is with you, they can walk while you stay beside them.

## You need
- A bed, sofa or chair to start from
- A sturdy chair to walk to, a few steps away
- A wall or heavy furniture to wedge that chair against
- Shoes or non-slip socks

## Camera
Prop your phone about 10 feet back, where it sees both seats and the floor between them.

## Safety
- Clear the floor first. Most falls at home are trips.
- Pause after you stand. If you feel dizzy, sit back down.
- With a real person, stay beside and slightly behind them, with one hand at the back of their waist. Never pull on an arm.

## Steps

### s1. Clear the path
Walk the path to the chair and move anything on the floor out of the way: shoes, bags, cords, loose rugs.
Pass when: the person walks or looks along the path and moves something aside, or sweeps a hand along it to show it is clear.
Try again when: something is left lying in the path.

### s2. Brace the chair you will walk to
Wedge it against a wall or heavy furniture, then push on it to check it will not slide.
Pass when: the person pushes or presses on the chair they will walk to, to test it.
Try again when: that chair is never tested.
Key step: yes

### s3. Put on shoes or grip socks
Put on shoes or non-slip socks. If they are already on, lift a foot to show the camera.
Pass when: shoes or non-slip socks go on, or a foot wearing them is lifted toward the camera.
Try again when: the walk is done in plain, slippery socks.

### s4. Stand up and pause
Stand up from your seat and hold still for a slow count of five before your first step.
Pass when: the person stands and stays still for several seconds before walking.
Try again when: the person walks off the moment they are up.
Key step: yes
After: s1 s2

### s5. Walk to the chair
Walk at a steady pace, looking ahead. Do not rush.
Pass when: the person walks to the chair at an even pace, without stumbling or grabbing at furniture.
Try again when: the person rushes, or leaves the cleared path.
After: s4

### s6. Turn, feel the chair, sit slowly
Turn around, back up until your legs touch the chair, reach back, and sit down slowly.
Pass when: the person backs up until the legs touch the chair, reaches back, and lowers with control.
Try again when: the person sits before the legs touch the chair, or drops into it.
Key step: yes
After: s5
