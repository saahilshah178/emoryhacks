# Shower assistance

## Setup
- A dry bathroom or kitchen with a tub or a taped line on the floor as the tub rim
- A sturdy chair as the transfer bench
- A walker, two chairs, or a counter to hold
- A belt or scarf as the transfer belt
- A towel, and non-slip socks
- This is a dry practice. No water in the tub.

## Safety
- Do not practice on a wet floor.
- Do not stand someone up from inside a real wet tub.
- Keep the belt on for standing. Take it off only when they are seated and centered.

## Steps

### s1. Clear and dry the bathroom floor
Pick up clutter. Wipe the floor dry with a towel even if it looks dry.
Say "done" when: the floor around the bench is clear and dry.

### s2. Back person up to tub bench
Using the walker or counter, turn them so they back up until both calves touch the chair.
Say "done" when: both legs touch the bench.

### s3. Lower person onto the tub bench
One hand reaches back for the seat, the other stays on the walker. Lower slowly. Move the walker aside after they sit.
Say "done" when: they are sitting and the walker is out of the way.

### s4. Move person's legs into the tub
Have them hold the bench, lift one leg at a time over the rim or tape line, then scoot toward the center.
Say "done" when: both legs are "in" and they are centered.

### s5. Remove the transfer belt
Unbuckle the belt only after they are seated and centered. Do not tug them off balance.
Say "done" when: the belt is off and they are still steady.

### s6. Move person's legs out of tub
Scoot to the outer edge. Lift each leg back over the rim. Turn to face out while still sitting. Do not stand from inside the tub.
Say "done" when: both feet are on the floor and they are still sitting.

### s7. Dry floor and check non-skid footwear
Wipe the floor by their feet. Confirm non-slip socks or shoes are on.
Say "done" when: the floor is dry and footwear is on.

### s8. Secure the transfer belt
Put the belt back on snugly and buckle it before standing.
Say "done" when: the belt is snug.

### s9. Position walker and prepare to stand
Place the walker in front. Stand close, feet apart, knees bent, back straight. One of their hands on the walker, the other pushing off the bench.
Say "done" when: walker, stance, and hands are set.

### s10. Count and assist person to stand
Count to three, help them stand with the belt, then pause to check balance before walking.
Say "done" when: they are standing and have paused.
