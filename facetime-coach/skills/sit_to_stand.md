# Sit to stand

Practice the cues that get someone out of a chair safely, by doing each one yourself. If someone is with you, they can sit while you give the cues.

## You need
- A sturdy chair that does not roll or swivel
- A wall to push the chair against
- Shoes or bare feet, not plain socks

## Camera
Prop your phone about 8 feet away at waist height, to the side of the chair, so the chair and your whole body show.

## Safety
- Use a chair that cannot roll, swivel or tip.
- Stop and sit down if you feel dizzy or anything hurts.
- With a real person, never pull them up by the arms or the neck.

## Steps

### s1. Brace the chair
Push the chair back against a wall, then press down on the seat to check it cannot slide.
Pass when: the chair is against a wall or other solid support, and the person pushes or presses on it to test it.
Try again when: the chair stands in open space where it could slide back, or it is never tested.
Key step: yes

### s2. Sit and hold the chair
Sit down and put both hands on the armrests, or on the sides of the seat.
Pass when: the person is seated with both hands on the armrests or the sides of the seat.
Try again when: the hands stay in the lap, or reach forward for something to pull on.
After: s1

### s3. Scoot to the front edge
Wriggle your hips forward until you sit on the front half of the seat.
Pass when: the hips move forward and the person ends up on the front half of the seat.
Try again when: the person stays sitting back against the backrest.
Key step: yes
After: s2

### s4. Tuck your feet back
Slide both feet back until they are under your knees, flat on the floor and hip-width apart.
Pass when: both feet slide back under the knees, flat on the floor and about hip-width apart.
Try again when: the feet stay out in front of the knees, or are pressed together.
After: s2

### s5. Lean forward, nose over toes
Look ahead, not at the floor, and lean your chest forward until your nose is over your toes.
Pass when: the upper body leans clearly forward over the feet before the person rises.
Try again when: the person tries to rise with the back straight up, or throws the body forward to get momentum.
After: s3

### s6. Stand up and pause
Push through your hands and feet to stand, then hold still for three seconds before you move.
Pass when: the person rises in one controlled move and then stands still for about three seconds.
Try again when: the person drops back into the chair, lurches up, or walks off at once.
Key step: yes
After: s5
