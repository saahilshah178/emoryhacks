# Fall response

## Setup
- A clear floor with two or three pillows
- A stuffed animal, large pillow, or backpack as the patient
- A phone within reach as the call bell
- Do not use a real person who might actually fall

## Safety
- Never practice a real fall with a person.
- Never try to catch someone and stand them back up.
- Never lift a fallen person from the floor by yourself.
- Keep your knees bent and your back straight.

## Steps

### s1. Support patient and ask how they feel
Walk up to the unsteady "patient," place hands on the upper arms of the pillow or stuffed animal, and ask if they are okay.
Say "done" when: you have both hands on the upper arms and have asked how they feel.

### s2. Position yourself closely behind the patient
Step directly behind, chest close to their back, so you can take weight.
Say "done" when: you are close behind them.

### s3. Support torso under arms or at waist
Reach under the arms or around the waist. Do not hold only the hands or clothing.
Say "done" when: you have a firm hold on the torso.

### s4. Place one leg under patient's buttocks
Step one foot forward so your thigh is under the buttocks. Widen your stance. Keep your back fairly straight.
Say "done" when: one thigh is under them and your knees are bent.

### s5. Lower patient gently down your leg
Bend your knees and slide the pillow slowly down your leg onto the floor. Protect the head. Do not drop it or pull it back up.
Say "done" when: they are sitting on the floor in a controlled way.

### s6. Stay with patient and check condition
Kneel beside them. Do not lift. Ask how they are.
Say "done" when: you are still beside them and have asked again.

### s7. Use call bell to summon help
Stay there. Tell them you are calling for help. Reach a phone or pretend call bell without walking away.
Say "done" when: you have called for help without leaving their side.
