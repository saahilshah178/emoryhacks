# Sit to stand

## Setup
- A sturdy chair, preferably with armrests
- A pillow on the chair if you are practicing solo
- Clear space in front of the chair
- Shoes or non-slip socks

## Safety
- Use a chair that will not roll.
- Do not pull a real person up by the arms or neck.
- If practicing alone, stand up yourself from the chair using the same cues.

## Steps

### s1. Greet patient and explain transfer
Stand beside the seated person (or sit in the chair if you are practicing alone). Greet them and say you will help them stand.
Say "done" when: they have heard the plan.

### s2. Instruct patient to hold armrests
Ask them to put both hands on the armrests. If there are no arms, use the sides of the seat.
Say "done" when: both hands have a stable hold.

### s3. Move patient to front of chair
Cue them to wriggle forward until they sit on the front edge. Help at the hips, not the arms.
Say "done" when: their hips are at the front edge.

### s4. Position feet back hip-width apart
Tuck feet back toward the chair, hip-width apart, so knees are over the feet.
Say "done" when: feet are back and about hip-width apart.

### s5. Support patient's shoulder and lower back
Stand beside them. One hand in front of the near shoulder, the other around the lower back. Do not grab the neck.
Say "done" when: your hands are in those two places.

### s6. Instruct patient to look forward and rock
Tell them to look forward, not at the floor. Practice a small rock on "ready" and "steady."
Say "done" when: they are looking forward and have rocked once.

### s7. Cue and assist patient to stand
Count "ready, steady, stand." Rock forward and stand together. Pause at the top to check balance.
Say "done" when: they are standing and steady.
