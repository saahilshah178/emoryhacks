# Assisted walking

## Setup
- A bed or sofa to start from
- A dining chair as the wheelchair destination
- A scarf or belt as the gait belt
- Non-slip socks or shoes
- A clear hallway path of a few steps

## Safety
- Stay close beside and slightly behind.
- Hold the belt the whole walk. Do not let go to open a door.
- If you live alone, walk the path yourself while holding a belt around a pillow, then talk through seating.

## Steps

### s1. Greet, sanitize hands, and explain procedure
Knock, clean your hands, greet them, and say you will walk to the chair.
Say "done" when: they have agreed to the walk.

### s2. Lock the bed brakes
If the bed cannot lock, push it against a wall and say "bed locked."
Say "done" when: the start surface will not slide.

### s3. Lock the wheelchair brakes
Brace the destination chair so it cannot move when they sit.
Say "done" when: the chair is stable.

### s4. Put non-skid slippers on patient
Put on non-slip socks or shoes before standing.
Say "done" when: both feet have grip.

### s5. Sit patient up and check dizziness
Help them sit on the edge. Feet flat. Ask if they feel dizzy and wait before standing.
Say "done" when: they are sitting, feet flat, and not dizzy.

### s6. Apply and secure the gait belt
Wrap the belt at the waist, buckle it, check two-finger snugness, and tuck the extra strap.
Say "done" when: the belt is snug and tucked.

### s7. Assist the patient to stand
Have them put hands on your shoulders, not your neck. Hold both sides of the belt. Count to three and stand.
Say "done" when: they are standing and you still hold the belt.

### s8. Walk alongside patient to wheelchair
Walk slightly behind and beside, one hand on the belt. Match their pace to the chair.
Say "done" when: you have reached the chair without letting go.

### s9. Seat patient safely in wheelchair
Turn so their back faces the chair, wait until the backs of the legs touch, hands on armrests, then lower on a count of three.
Say "done" when: they are sitting with control.

### s10. Remove belt, give call light, sanitize
Remove the belt, hand them a phone or remote, put the belt away, and clean your hands.
Say "done" when: belt is off, help is in reach, and hands are clean.
