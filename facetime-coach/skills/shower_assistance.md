# Shower assistance

Practice getting onto a shower seat and over a tub edge the safe way: sit first, then lift the legs over. This is dry practice in any room, and you do the move yourself.

## You need
- A sturdy chair with no wheels, as the shower seat
- A strip of tape on the floor about a foot in front of the chair, as the tub edge
- A towel
- Bare feet or non-slip socks

## Camera
Prop your phone about 8 feet away, to the side, so the chair, the tape line and your feet all show.

## Safety
- Dry practice only. No water and no wet floor.
- With a real person, nobody steps over a tub edge standing up. Sit first, then legs.
- Never stand someone up inside a wet tub.

## Steps

### s1. Dry the floor
Wipe the floor around the chair with the towel, even if it looks dry, and move any mat out of the way.
Pass when: the person wipes the floor by the chair with a towel.
Try again when: the floor is never wiped.

### s2. Back up and sit
Back up until your legs touch the chair, reach back for the seat, and sit down slowly.
Pass when: the person backs up until the legs touch the chair, reaches back, and lowers with control.
Try again when: the person sits before the legs touch the chair, or drops onto the seat.
Key step: yes
After: s1

### s3. Lift your legs over the edge
Stay seated. Lift one leg at a time over the tape line, as high as a tub edge.
Pass when: while seated, each leg is lifted over the line, one at a time.
Try again when: the person stands up to step over the line, or drags both legs over together.
Key step: yes
After: s2

### s4. Bring your legs back out
Still seated, lift each leg back over the line and plant both feet flat on the floor.
Pass when: while seated, each leg comes back over the line one at a time and both feet end flat on the floor.
Try again when: the person stands up while a foot is still past the line.
After: s3

### s5. Dry your feet and the floor
Before you stand, wipe your feet and the floor in front of the chair with the towel.
Pass when: the seated person wipes their feet or the floor in front of the chair with the towel.
Try again when: the person stands without drying anything.
After: s4

### s6. Stand up and pause
Push up from the seat, stand, and hold still for three seconds before you walk.
Pass when: the person pushes up from the seat, stands, and stays still for about three seconds.
Try again when: the person walks off at once, or stands while a foot is still past the line.
Key step: yes
After: s4
