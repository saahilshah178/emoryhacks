# Vehicle transfer

Practice getting into a car seat the safe way: sit first, then bring the legs in. Two chairs stand in for the wheelchair and the car, and you do the move yourself.

## You need
- A sturdy chair to start from, as the wheelchair
- A second chair without arms, as the car seat. Its side is the open car door
- Clear floor between the two chairs
- Shoes or bare feet, not plain socks

## Camera
Prop your phone about 8 feet away, so both chairs and your whole body show.

## Safety
- Sit first, then swing the legs in. Never step into a car one foot first.
- Duck your head as you sit, as you would under a door frame.
- Never lift a person who cannot stand on their own.
- If you use a real car instead, park on level ground with the engine off.

## Steps

### s1. Set the two seats close
Put your chair next to the side of the car seat, angled toward it, with room to stand between them.
Pass when: the starting chair ends up within one step of the side of the car seat, angled toward it.
Try again when: the chair is left more than a step away, or facing away.

### s2. Lock the brakes
Push on your chair to check it will not slide. Wedge it against a wall or furniture if it does.
Pass when: the person pushes or presses on the starting chair to test it.
Try again when: the chair is never tested.
Key step: yes

### s3. Scoot forward and stand
Sit in your chair, scoot to the front edge, put your feet flat, then stand up and pause.
Pass when: the person moves to the front of the seat, then stands fully and is steady.
Try again when: the person lurches up from the back of the seat, or never comes to a full stand.
After: s1 s2

### s4. Back up until legs touch the seat
Turn with small steps and back up until the backs of both legs touch the side of the car seat.
Pass when: the person turns with small steps and backs up until the backs of the legs touch the car seat.
Try again when: the person sits before the legs touch the seat, or twists at the waist instead of stepping.
Key step: yes
After: s3

### s5. Sit down first
Reach back, duck your head and sit down sideways on the seat, with both feet still on the floor.
Pass when: the person sits down on the car seat with control while both feet are still on the floor outside it.
Try again when: a foot or leg goes in first, or the person drops onto the seat.
Key step: yes
After: s4

### s6. Swing your legs in
Lift one leg in, then the other, turning on the seat until you face the front of the chair.
Pass when: after sitting, the legs are lifted in one at a time and the person ends up facing the front of the seat.
Try again when: both legs are swung in together in a rush, or the person never turns to face the front.
After: s5
