# Vehicle transfer

## Setup
- A car with the front passenger door free, or a dining chair beside a sofa as the car seat
- A second chair as the wheelchair
- A belt or scarf as the gait belt
- Non-slip shoes

## Safety
- Park on level ground and turn the car off if you use a real car.
- Do not lift someone who cannot stand.
- Watch your head on the door frame.
- If you have no car, say so and use the sofa as the seat.

## Steps

### s1. Open car door and slide seat back
Open the door fully. Slide the passenger seat back, or pull the sofa cushion back to make space.
Say "done" when: the opening is as wide as it can be.

### s2. Position wheelchair close to car seat
Angle the chair toward the seat so the pivot is short. Leave room to stand.
Say "done" when: the chair is close and angled to the seat.

### s3. Lock the wheelchair brakes
Lock brakes, or brace the chair so it cannot slide.
Say "done" when: the chair is stable.

### s4. Swing away or remove footrests
Move footrests, bags, or shoes so both feet can plant on the ground.
Say "done" when: feet have a clear path.

### s5. Have patient scoot to chair edge
Cue them to scoot forward until their weight is over their feet.
Say "done" when: they are on the front edge.

### s6. Assist patient to stand using gait belt
Hold the belt, keep your back straight, and count to three as you stand together.
Say "done" when: they are standing with the belt in your hands.

### s7. Pivot patient until legs touch car
Take small steps until the backs of their legs touch the car seat or sofa.
Say "done" when: both legs are against the seat.

### s8. Lower patient onto car seat
Have them reach back for a stable surface and sit slowly. Guide with the belt.
Say "done" when: they are sitting in the seat.

### s9. Lift patient's legs into the car
Help them scoot back, then lift one leg at a time into the footwell, or onto the sofa as if into a car.
Say "done" when: they face forward with both legs in.
